Common tool questions

Which tool should I use for fish farming?

Use the Fish Farm Planner to filter by zone and rank candidates by meat per day and breeding rate.

How do I choose the best recipes?

Use the Recipe Filter with a max taste threshold and dish count target to match your ingredient supply.

How do I compare staff?

Use Staff Compare to review base and max Cooking, Serving, Procurement and Appeal stats side by side.
