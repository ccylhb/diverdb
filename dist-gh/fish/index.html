Fishing data FAQ

What is the best fish to farm?

Rank candidates by meat per day and breeding rate in the Fish Farm Planner before choosing ranch stock.

Why do fish appear in multiple zones?

Many fish can appear in more than one Blue Hole zone and at different active times.

How do I find nocturnal fish?

Unlock night diving through A Noisy Customer, then filter this database by the night active time.
