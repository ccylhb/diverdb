Best Fish for Early Game

Early game success comes from consistent catches, not rare drops. These shallow-water fish give solid meat output and appear in recipes that keep the menu full.

Why shallow fish matter

Shallow dives are fast, cheap and repeatable. Fish from this zone feed early recipes, fill the farm and build the Cooksta rank that unlocks restaurant upgrades. Prioritize species with high meat per day once the farm opens.

Restaurant priority

Keep a mix of high-volume fish and event fish. The farm should feed daily recipes, while the dive should chase VIP ingredients and boss drops. Use the Fish Farm Planner when deciding what to stock.
