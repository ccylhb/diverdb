Tuna Party & Salvage Drone Unlock

Tuna becomes available after the seasonal Tuna Party, which also gives you access to the Salvage Drone.

How the event unlocks

Seasonal events are calendar-driven and appear after the Jellyfish Party around October 12. The Tuna Party arrives through the Mail app after the Jellyfish Party. Cobra gives access to the Salvage Drone, which is useful for transporting large fish like tuna.

The event marks qualifying dishes with a pink bow tie icon. Stock tuna ingredients before the party and save the right recipes so the menu can run through the event night.

After the party

Cobra's shop can sell the Steel Net Sensor Trap once the Tuna Party is announced. Net weapons and the drone make tuna farming much easier, so upgrade the drone and net gun before the next event.
