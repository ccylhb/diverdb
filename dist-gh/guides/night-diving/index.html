Night Diving & Nocturnal Fish

Night diving unlocks through A Noisy Customer and is required for many nocturnal catches.

How to unlock night diving

Start the Chapter 2 sub mission A Noisy Customer. Bancho asks for Moray Eel Curry, and moray eels only appear at night. Starting this mission opens the night dive option in the evening schedule.

Why night fish matter

Several high-rank fish, recipe ingredients and Marinca cards only spawn at night. Plan night dives around specific targets instead of general farming.
