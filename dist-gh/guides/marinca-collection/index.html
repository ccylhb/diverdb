Marinca Card Collection Guide

Marinca records fish and creature cards for each Blue Hole zone.

How the collection works

Marinca is the in-game fish encyclopedia. Each catch fills a card with rank, length and zone information. Reaching higher ranks on a fish card often requires catching larger or higher-grade versions of the same species.

The paid expansion In The Jungle adds Marinca Bloom, unlocked by Sato after he arrives in the Jungle. It adds jungle cards and a flower that grows from sunlight, water and Marinca progress.
