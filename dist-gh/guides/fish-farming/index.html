Best Fish to Farm for the Ranch

Ranch value is not about the biggest fish. It is about stable daily meat output.

What makes a good farm fish

The Marinca ranch rewards species with high meat per day, a reasonable breeding rate and enough breed cap to keep production running. Rare fish with enormous size are usually worse farm animals because they occupy capacity for lower daily yield.

Ranch strategy

Start with one or two species that match your current restaurant ingredients. Keep the farm lean until you unlock more tank capacity, then add the best producers from the table above. Use the Fish Farm Planner to filter by zone and minimum meat per day as you unlock new regions.
