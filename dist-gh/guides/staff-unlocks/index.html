Staff Unlocks & Recruitment

Staff members arrive through contracts, mission progress and restaurant ranks.

How recruitment works

New employees become available as the story and Cooksta rank progress. Hiring costs vary by staff member, and each employee can be trained to raise Cooking, Serving, Procurement and Appeal. Some staff unlock new recipes at specific training levels.

Training priority

Train the staff member with the highest max ceiling for the bottleneck role. If the kitchen stacks up, prioritize Cooking; if tables wait, prioritize Serving. Use the Staff Compare tool to test two candidates.
