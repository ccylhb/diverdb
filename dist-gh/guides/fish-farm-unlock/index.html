How to Unlock the Fish Farm

The Fish Farm opens after the Chapter 2 sub mission A Noisy Customer.

Unlock steps

  1. Reach Chapter 2 and wait for Bancho's call about a noisy customer.
  2. Dive at night to catch a moray eel.
  3. Research and serve Moray Eel Curry.
  4. Talk to Otto after the restaurant closes.
  5. Meet Otto at the empty lot behind Bancho Sushi.

Starting this mission also unlocks Night Diving, which is required for nocturnal fish and several achievements.

What the farm does

The farm has sections for each Blue Hole biome. Otto sells tank upgrades and new area unlocks. Two fish of the same species can breed, and caught eggs or roe can be sent to the farm when tank space is available.
