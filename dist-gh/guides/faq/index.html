Dave the Diver FAQ

Fast answers to the most common Dave the Diver questions.

How do I unlock the Fish Farm in Dave the Diver?

Complete the Chapter 2 sub mission A Noisy Customer, which requires serving Moray Eel Curry.

Full guide

When do tuna fish appear?

Tuna becomes available after the seasonal Tuna Party is announced through the Mail app.

Full guide

What is the best fish to farm?

Species with high meat per day and a reasonable breed cap are best. Use the Fish Farm Planner to rank them.

Full guide

How do I upgrade weapons?

Use Duff's Weapon Shop for guns and the iDiver app for diving equipment.

Full guide

How do I start the Jungle DLC?

In the Jungle adds Sato and Marinca Bloom, which tracks jungle fish cards.
